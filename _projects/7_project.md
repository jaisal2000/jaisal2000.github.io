## Overview

A deep learning-based image colorization method utilizing Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to automatically colorize grayscale images.

### Key Contributions

- **GAN-based Colorization**: Proposed and implemented a generative adversarial network for realistic image colorization
- **Multi-Color Space Study**: Researched various color models including LAB, YUV, and HSI colorspaces for colorization effectiveness
- **Published Research**: This work was published at ICACS 2025 and received the Best Presentation Award

### Technologies Used
- PyTorch
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
- OpenCV
- Deep Learning
